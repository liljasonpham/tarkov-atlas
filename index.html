Open the raid planner Story chapters

Start from a map

Opens the planner on that map with your selected quests.

The map tells you what's on each floor

Pick a floor and everything else greys out. Turn on extracts, rare loot, tech, meds or safes. Boss spawn chances sit in the corner, and objectives without a pin stay listed so you don't forget the kill counts.

Your packing list writes itself

Keys, markers and quest items for the quests you picked on this map, with shared keys counted once. A heads-up lists the found-in-raid items you'll need for quests that unlock soon, so you keep them when you find them.

Story chapters, one step at a time

Click the step you're on and everything before it is marked done. Steps with a location have a Map button that drops you on the pin.

Quest data, coordinates and spawn chances from tarkov.dev. Guides and photos from the Escape from Tarkov Wiki. Map art from tarkov.dev contributors (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).
